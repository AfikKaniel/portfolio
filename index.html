amp

Senior Product Manager

2025–2026

amp is a connected-fitness startup. I joined to bring game-style engagement to strength training, and led an AI-driven product built around personalization.

  • AI coach. It builds a personal workout that people can still adjust by hand. 68% of users use it in every session.
  • Real-time scoring. Every rep gets a quality grade, and the weight adjusts itself from performance data.
  • AI voice engine. Live feedback in the coach's voice, triggered by what happens in the workout.
  • Social and music. Shared Workouts, and amp Music with Spotify and Apple Music.

The result: a personal, interactive trainer with industry-leading engagement and user satisfaction.

Product Team Lead

I led 3 product managers and 6 development teams, with a focus on studio workflow and R&D efficiency.

  • Data. Rebuilt the data infrastructure for more precise tracking and better product decisions.

SuperPlay

Product Manager

2022–2023

I joined SuperPlay early to build its core product experiences, and designed a feature roadmap that covers the whole player lifecycle.

  • The Album. A 3-month collection system built on the completionist mindset. +38% revenue.
  • The PET. A 4-week progression system built on social status and collection, launched one week before each Album ends. +25% revenue, and it became a permanent core feature.
  • The Pixies, my own invention. Time-limited double rewards with short "mini-completion" goals, aimed at the mid-cycle dip. +33% revenue, and 6 major competitors adopted it.

The result: three features that each grew revenue by 25–38%. The Pixies, which I invented, became an industry benchmark.

Playtika

Monetization Operations, then Product Manager

2020–2022

I joined Playtika in early 2020, straight from military leadership. My studio made $2.5M a day but leaned on a small group of VIP players, so I set out to grow the paying base.

  • Store redesign. Moved the daily bonus so players see personal bundles and deals more often.
  • Time-limited coupons. Automated, personal coupons that use urgency and the endowment effect.
  • Offer design. Decoy offers to steer purchases, rolling offers for purchase momentum, and post-purchase mini-games.

The result: a much larger paying user base, and ARPDAU above 1.7¢.
